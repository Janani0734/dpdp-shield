1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickCart, an online grocery delivery app. By using our app you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, device identifiers, precise location and payment details when you register and place orders. We also collect your browsing activity inside the app.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to process orders and deliver them, to personalise offers, to send marketing messages and to improve our services. We may share your information with delivery partners, advertisers and analytics providers for our business purposes.

4. Who Can Use QuickCart

Our services are available to users of all ages.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as necessary for our business needs.

6. Security

We use encryption for payment details and take reasonable steps to protect your other information.

7. Your Choices

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you by emailing support. You can update your profile details in the app. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the email.

8. Contact Us

For any questions write to support@quickcart.example.

9. Changes

We may change this policy from time to time.
