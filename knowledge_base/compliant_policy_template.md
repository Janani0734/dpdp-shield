## Template 1 - Notice of data collected and purpose
List each category of personal data you collect, the specific purpose for each, and the service it enables. State that you collect only what is necessary, and where the Data Principal can read this notice in English or any Eighth Schedule language.

## Template 2 - Consent and withdrawal
We process your personal data only with your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent, given by a clear affirmative action. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, from Settings or by writing to [grievance email]. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before you withdrew.

## Template 3 - Children
We collect personal data from children (under 18) only with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian obtained before processing begins. We do not track or behaviourally monitor children and do not show them targeted advertising.

## Template 4 - Retention and erasure
We keep personal data only as long as the purpose is served, which is [N] months after account closure, or longer where law requires. We then erase it and instruct our processors to do the same.

## Template 5 - Breach notification
If a personal data breach occurs we will without delay tell each affected user what happened, the likely consequences, what we have done and what they can do, and we will intimate the Data Protection Board of India, giving full details within 72 hours. We report cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours.

## Template 6 - Your rights and grievance redressal
You can access a summary of your data, correct or update it, request erasure, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Contact our Grievance Officer: [Name, designation, email, phone]. If we do not resolve your grievance, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
