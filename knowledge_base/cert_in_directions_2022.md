## Direction 1 - Report cyber incidents within 6 hours
Service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates and government organisations must report specified cyber security incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing the incident or of it being brought to their notice. Reportable incidents include data breaches, data leaks, unauthorised access to IT systems or data, ransomware, attacks on critical systems, and compromise of servers or applications. Reports go to incident@cert-in.org.in, with details such as the incident type, time, affected systems and symptoms.

## Direction 2 - Point of contact
Entities must designate a point of contact to interface with CERT-In and communicate the details to CERT-In.

## Direction 3 - Log retention
Logs of all ICT systems must be enabled and retained securely for a rolling period of 180 days within Indian jurisdiction, and provided to CERT-In on request.

## Direction 4 - Clock synchronisation
All ICT system clocks must be synchronised to the NTP servers of NIC or NPL, or servers traceable to them.

## Relationship with the DPDP Act
CERT-In reporting (6 hours, cyber incidents) is separate from and in addition to the DPDP breach duties (intimating affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board, with detailed information to the Board within 72 hours). A single incident can trigger both.
